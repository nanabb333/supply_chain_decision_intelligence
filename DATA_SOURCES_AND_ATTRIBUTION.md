# Data sources and attribution

## Provenance

The underlying project analyzed historical retail data from the M5 Forecasting competition through the [organizers' Mcompetitions/M5-methods repository](https://github.com/Mcompetitions/M5-methods) and its linked archive. The recorded inputs were `sales_train_validation.csv` (daily units sold), `calendar.csv` (calendar/event attributes), and `sell_prices.csv` (weekly selling prices). These filenames identify inputs; no input files or data subsets are distributed here.

The project's accepted acquisition receipt records the following file sources:

- [Historical sales file](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w5nrvtdZlIAsYdCHSmXoCq4rTzmhcuPL/view)
- [Calendar file](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eeU8Me44yzgWsDD0dYrX4xVOixil_H4u/view)
- [Weekly selling-price file](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YKo2D7rpg9y5L-qaidUjyJaKvTIR0rMI/view)

These are recorded source locations, not permission grants or guarantees of continued accessibility. Sales are observed quantities sold, not unconstrained demand. Weekly-price revenue estimates are not audited transaction receipts. Inventory results in the README are hypothetical simulations, not observed retailer operating outcomes.

## Rights limitations

Public availability does not establish permission to redistribute data or derived content. The [official M5 competition rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy/rules) were identified in the prior licensing assessment, but their text was not retrievable through the review tool. No applicable redistribution grant was established.

Access to data, analysis of data, redistribution of raw/processed data, and publication of derived figures, reports or numerical summaries are separate questions. Their applicable conditions remain unresolved for the intended public portfolio use. Omitting raw data does not itself establish permission to publish derived findings. This document does not assert either legal clearance or a confirmed violation.

The current candidate includes only two Markdown documents. It contains no dataset, derived image, report PDF, code, notebook, dashboard or ledger. Before external publication, the author must resolve applicable terms for the intended derived-result narrative and separately approve the exact payload and publishing action. No LICENSE file or third-party rights grant is supplied.
